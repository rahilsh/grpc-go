# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and temporarily
or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful. Report abuse through GitHub's content reporting tools.
All reports will be reviewed and investigated as promptly and fairly as
possible. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.
